This project was born out of necessity, built in solitude, and released for anyone who needs a quiet place to heal.

Role

Solo Developer

The Creator

Meet Bill

I'm a disabled veteran, a software developer, and a man in long-term recovery. For years, I searched for a digital companion that respected my privacy as much as it respected my journey.

Most apps wanted my email, my data, or a subscription fee. They felt like businesses first and tools second.

So I built Recovery & Renewal. No investors. No tracking. No paywalls. Just a tool I use every day, shared with you.

Built in Lewiston, Maine
Veteran Owned

Radical Privacy

Your recovery data is yours alone. This app has no servers, no analytics, and no login screens. If you delete the app, your data vanishes forever.

Offline First

Recovery doesn't always happen where the Wi-Fi is good. The entire app—devotionals, tools, and resources—works 100% offline.

Always Free

You can't put a price on sobriety. The core app will always be free. If you want to support development, you can, but you never have to.

Under The Hood

How is it built?

Built with React Native and Expo for a smooth, native experience on Android (and soon iOS). The UI uses a custom design system focused on calm, dark-mode aesthetics to reduce eye strain.

Why open source?

Trust is earned. By making the code public on GitHub, anyone can verify that we aren't tracking you. It also allows the recovery community to contribute improvements.

How can I help?

Spread the word. If you're a developer, check out the GitHub repo. If you're just a fan, consider buying me a coffee to keep the servers (well, the website hosting) running.
