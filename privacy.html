Radical Privacy

Recovery requires honesty. Honesty requires safety. Safety requires privacy.

The "Too Long; Didn't Read" Version

  • No Accounts: You don't create a login. We don't know who you are.
  • No Cloud: All your journal entries, milestones, and settings live on your phone.
  • No Tracking: We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, or crash trackers.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. The Recovery & Renewal app is "offline-first." This means that when you write a journal entry or save a milestone, that data is written to your device's internal storage. It never leaves your phone.

2. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate with third-party advertising networks or analytics providers. There is no code in our app that "phones home" to tell us how you use it.

3. Data Deletion

Since we do not have your data, we cannot delete it for you. However, you have full control. If you uninstall the app, your data is destroyed by your operating system. There is no backup on our servers because there are no servers.

4. Contacting Us

If you email us for support, we will see your email address. We use this solely to reply to your question. We do not build mailing lists, and we will never sell your contact info.
